SuXXES – Sustainability Extension for XES Event Logs

SuXXES is a XES extension that enables a uniform integration of sustainability-related data into case-centric event logs. It considers all three dimensions of sustainability: economic, environmental, and social.

This extension is associated with a scientific publication (accepted, forthcoming 2026, Springer Nature Switzerland AG). The .xesext definition file is released independently under CC BY 4.0.

Dimensions

SuXXES covers the following 10 sustainability dimensions at the event level:

💼 Economic

DimensionKeyDescription
Sustainability ManagementsmanagementSustainability certificates and initiatives associated with the business process

🌍 Environmental

DimensionKeyDescription
EnergyenergyEnergy used in the business process
EmissionemissionEmissions generated by the business process
WaterwaterWater usage of the business process
WastewasteWaste produced in the business process
MaterialmaterialMaterials used in the business process

👥 Social

DimensionKeyDescription
EmployeeemployeeEmployee-related information of the business process
WorkplaceworkplaceWorkplace-related information of the business process
SafetysafetyAccidents and safety incidents in the business process
StakeholderstakeholderStakeholder satisfaction related to the business process

Extension File

The SuXXES extension definition file (.xesext) is available here:

📄 SuXXES.xesext

To reference this extension in your XES event log, add the following extension declaration:

<extension name="SuXXES"
           prefix="suxxes"
           uri="https://nathalietum.github.io/SuXXES/suxxes.xesext"/>

Example Event Logs

Example event logs applying the SuXXES extension are publicly available on Zenodo:

Wolf, N., Wittges, H., Rinderle-Ma, S. (2025).
SuXXES Event Logs: Synthetic and ERP-Based Process Data with Integrated Sustainability Data.
Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17756035

The dataset contains four event logs covering diverse scenarios:

LogTypeProcess

All logs comply with the IEEE 1849-2023 XES standard and are validated against the official XML schema.
